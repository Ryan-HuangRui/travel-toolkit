# Travel Culture Card

Read [the card contract](references/card-contract.md) before producing a card.

## Inputs

Use the Trip Buddy item and the referenced `travel/trip.json` to understand geographic relevance. The card itself must stand independently: it does not explain why it was selected today or expose a route, booking, time, or private location.

## Workflow

1. Choose one place, work, person, craft, or historical turn. Avoid repeating a recent item or a work already central to another queued card.
2. Verify the factual spine with at least two primary or institutional sources. A travel publisher may guide broad editorial structure but is not a factual source.
3. Produce a local Markdown source first. Add a visual only when it materially supports looking; favour properly attributed institution or public-domain material.
4. Return a delivery candidate to Trip Buddy: title, local path, source URLs, freshness `durable`, and a one-sentence notification hook. Do not create a document or send a message yourself.
