# Culture Card Contract

- Story first: begin with a visible detail, a person, or a decisive moment; use only the historical facts that deepen it.
- Chinese prose should be concrete and economical. Avoid generic praise, manufactured lyricism, “must-see” language, and checklist framing.
- A short `参观指引` is optional. It offers ways to notice, compare, or pause; it is never a timetable or route.
- Include `来源` with at least two direct URLs to museums, archives, city/heritage bodies, artist estates, or comparable institutions.
- The title should identify the work or idea rather than the scheduled day. Countdown labels are a rendering choice owned by Trip Buddy, not the cultural source.
- Generated images may be used only as a labelled atmosphere image, never as a fake artwork, historical reconstruction, source record, or replacement for a real work.
