# Daily Brief Contract

Suggested compact structure:

1. `今天的锚点` — confirmed times and places only.
2. `出门前` — forecast, clothing, rain/sun/wind, tickets/documents, or access items that change behaviour.
3. `留意` — a venue or transport constraint supported by a current source.
4. `备选` — a concrete fallback when a weather-sensitive or fragile plan is affected.

Forecast language must name the place and local date. It expires at the end of that local day; it is not durable itinerary evidence.
