# Disruption Alert Contract

Use `alert` only if all apply:

- current official/direct source;
- specific itinerary place and date/time;
- material effect on a confirmed/accepted activity, reservation, transfer, or safety;
- concise impact statement and fallback;
- a stated validity window.

Use `daily_brief` for manageable weather, queues, or operating notes. Use `no_action` for unverified, expired, irrelevant, or non-material findings. The purpose is decision support, not an anxious news feed.
